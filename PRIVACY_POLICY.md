# Privacy Policy for YouTube Bookmarker

**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

YouTube Bookmarker ("the extension") lets you save and organize timestamped bookmarks while watching videos on YouTube.

## Data We Collect

The extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not have a backend server, and no data ever leaves your browser.

The only information the extension stores is:

- **Bookmarks you create** — video title, video ID/URL, timestamp, and any category or note you add.
- **Your settings** — preferences such as theme and keyboard shortcuts.

This data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's `storage.local` API.

## How We Use Permissions

- **`storage`** — to save your bookmarks and settings on your device.
- **`tabs` / `scripting` / `webNavigation`** — to detect when you're watching a YouTube video and to add the bookmarking controls to the page.
- **Host permission for `youtube.com`** — required so the extension can run on YouTube pages and read the current video's information (title, ID, timestamp) to create bookmarks.

None of this information is sent to us or to any third party.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to anyone. There are no analytics, tracking, or advertising services embedded in the extension.

## Data Storage & Retention

All data stays in your browser's local storage until you delete it (by removing bookmarks in the extension or uninstalling the extension, which clears its storage).
